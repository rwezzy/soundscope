SOUNDSCOPE SENSOR CARRIER v2 — STATIONARY TRIPOD ASSEMBLY

Use PRINT_ON_SV06.txt for the supplied complete twelve-part SV06 PLA project.
Physical fit, printed-thread function, grip, actual USB plugs and tripod
stability remain unverified. This is not a wrist or moving-robot mount.
Do not substitute metal screws in the custom printed threads.
ASSEMBLY ON THE BENCH
1. Unplug both sensors. Keep the camera supported on a soft surface. Remove
   it from its tripod while fitting the surrounding carrier.
2. Slide the two SQUARE PRINTED NUTS into the front-opening pockets at the
   top of the camera frame. Each screw hole points up/down, not toward the lens.
3. With the pressure bar removed, fit the camera into the lower curved seats.
   The narrow side-band front edge sits about 6 mm behind the camera's front
   face. The wide rear heatsink/USB shoulders remain behind the clamp band.
   Do not push the holder back until it catches the rear USB shoulders.
4. Place the pressure bar over the camera, profiled face toward its housing.
   Feed the two LONG PRINTED screws downward through the roof nuts until
   their tips meet the bar's shallow cups. Alternate small finger turns only
   until the camera does not wiggle. No pliers, wrench or metal substitute.
5. While supporting the camera over padding, gently check left/right, up/down
   and fore/aft movement. The fore/aft hold relies on clamp friction. If it
   slides, do not keep adding force: the contact fit needs revision.
6. Reattach the original tripod directly to the CAMERA's original threaded
   hole. The print does not go between the tripod head and camera. Check that
   the tripod head fits the 60 mm-wide open underside and spreads stably.
7. Rest the mic PCB on its three rim supports, main USB edge UP and LED /
   microphone-inlet side forward, the same direction the camera looks.
   Retainer 1 goes upper right, 2 upper left, 3 lower left. Each curved tongue
   points toward the board centre. Fit the three SHORT PRINTED screws from
   the front, through the retainer holes into the printed frame bosses.
   Tighten gently until play stops; the bare board must stay flat.
8. Connect both USB leads. The camera has rear-open, sloping side notches for
   its plug body. The mic's main upper USB and rear components remain open.
   Your actual cable overmolds have not been measured: dry-fit both plugs
   without force and arrange slack so cables cannot pull or tip the tripod.

BEFORE USING SOUNDSCOPE
This changes the relative sensor positions. Redo microphone-to-camera
calibration after the devices are rigidly secured. Do not reuse the old offsets
and assume the print automatically updates them. No software config was changed.
