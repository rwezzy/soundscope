PRINT THE COMPLETE SENSOR HOLDER ON THE IEEE SOVOL SV06

Open SoundScope_SV06_PLA_FULL.3mf in PrusaSlicer as a PROJECT, not geometry only.
It contains all 12 pieces, their layout, and the printer/filament/print settings.
No separate fit-check print is required for this job.

This is the stationary tripod carrier for the Gemini 2 and bare reSpeaker.
It is not an arm/wrist attachment. Assembly is in READ_BEFORE_PRINTING.txt;
the old makerspace settings and test-first print sequence in that older guide
do not apply to this SV06 full-print project.

1. Open the 3MF as a project. Confirm printer is the original Sovol SV06,
   0.4 mm nozzle, and the loaded spool actually says PLA. This is not an SV06
   Plus/ACE preset. If the spool is PETG, select its proper filament profile
   and re-slice; do not print PETG with the saved PLA temperatures.
2. Keep scale at 100% and the supplied flat orientations. No resizing.
3. Settings already saved: 0.20 mm layers, 0.20 mm first layer, three walls,
   four top/bottom layers, 15% gyroid infill, no supports, 5 mm outer brim,
   0.50 mm first-layer line width. Default Generic PLA: 215 C first nozzle
   layer, 210 C after; 60 C bed. Check the spool's temperature range.
4. Click Slice now. The checked project estimates 3 h 30 min and 31.40 g on
   PrusaSlicer 2.9.7. Actual machine time can differ. The earlier 69-minute
   estimate was for a different, faster printer and is not valid here.
5. Export ordinary .gcode to the printer's memory card, eject the card, then
   insert it into the SV06. Do not copy the STL/3MF and expect it to print.
6. Follow the printer owner's established loading/bed-preparation procedure.
   Select the exported job using its control knob. Watch the first layer;
   stop if pieces detach or plastic gathers around the nozzle.
7. Let the plate cool, remove all 12 pieces, and remove the thin brim borders.
   Fit the printed screws gently by hand before installing the electronics.

215.40 x 214.82 mm, within the 220 x 220 mm bed. Project is centered.
